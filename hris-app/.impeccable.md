## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are HR/Admin staff managing employee records and compliance documents inside a day-to-day operational workflow. They need quick access during repetitive administrative tasks.

### Brand Personality
Professional, calm, compact. The interface should feel trustworthy, efficient, and focused on clarity over decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a minimalist enterprise style inspired by Google Drive information density: strong scannability, compact controls, clear hierarchy, and restrained visual effects. Keep existing neutral palette with orange as a limited accent color. Avoid AI-looking card-heavy or overly decorative layouts.

### Design Principles
1. Optimize for fast scanning and retrieval before visual flourish.
2. Prefer compact, information-dense layouts with clear alignment and spacing rhythm.
3. Use neutral surfaces and borders; reserve orange for active/important states only.
4. Keep motion subtle and minimal to support focus and reduce distraction.
5. Reuse existing UI components and established patterns over one-off styling.
